WELCOME TO WILLOW'S

Homemade food with heart.

Willow's Wildflowers is a small home-based micro bakery serving Chillicothe, Ohio and surrounding areas.

Everything is made in small batches with a homemade, old-fashioned feel. Our menu changes with the seasons, so there's always something new coming from the kitchen.
